05
Connection

Meet us from a supported client.

Cross-platform

Cross-Platform Access

Geyser and Floodgate provide the configured Bedrock-to-Java connection path. Version support follows the versions supported by Geyser; individual client or account setups may still need staff help.

Skins

Skin Support

SkinsRestorer, Floodgate, and BedrockSkinRestorer are installed. What appears can depend on the client and current configuration; contact staff if your skin does not show correctly.

Support

Help when something goes wrong.

Staff tools

Cottage Recovery

Staff can inspect inventory backups and restore saved data when appropriate. Recovery is staff-managed, not a self-service guarantee.

07
Teams & community

Build together and stay connected.

Teams

Team System

UltimateTeams supports team creation, invitations, members and managers, shared homes and warps, team chat, allies and enemies, friendly-fire controls, and team ender chests. Limits follow the server configuration.

Discord Bridge

Discord Bridge

DiscordSRV connects the configured Discord and Minecraft chat channels. If messages do not appear where expected, contact staff.

Shared space

Keep the backyard nice.

The community rules apply here too. Minecraft-specific rules are handled separately through mcrules?.

The Cottage★ is built around people first. Treat the people in the backyard with the same respect you would give them in the main home.
Respect everyone

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or targeted behavior.

Keep it appropriate

No sexual, excessively disturbing, or otherwise inappropriate content.

No disruption

Don't intentionally disrupt the community, spam, abuse mentions, or misuse server features.

Protect privacy

Don't share private information, screenshots, conversations, or personal details without permission.

No malicious content

No malware, phishing, scams, malicious links, or attempts to compromise another person's account.

Play fair

Follow the Minecraft rules listed under mcrules?.

Enforcement: Depending on the situation, breaking the rules may result in a warning, timeout, kick, or ban.
