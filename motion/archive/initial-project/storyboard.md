# The Cottage★ — Motion Design Storyboard

**Status:** Scene artwork and editable vector layouts are in this repository. The final video renders and animation source project are not complete yet.

## Creative direction

The Cottage★ is presented as the home: a private, people-first community. The SMP appears as the shared backyard — an activity that belongs to the wider community story, not the identity of the whole project.

The visual direction uses the website palette and a warm editorial style with crisp typography, soft rounded cards, geometric transitions, and restrained motion.

## 45-second sequence

| Timecode | Duration | Scene | Motion direction |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 00:00–00:05 | 5s | Opening — The Cottage★ | Paper field resolves into the dark title card; cottage seal draws on; star settles into place. |
| 00:05–00:11 | 6s | From different places | Profile circles drift in from separate edges; dotted paths connect them; message cards pop in with gentle overshoot. |
| 00:11–00:17 | 6s | One little home | Friend circles orbit toward the centre; the cottage mark becomes the visual anchor; title fades up in two beats. |
| 00:17–00:24 | 7s | Our corner | Community interface slides into view; channel labels and message cards reveal in a staggered sequence. |
| 00:24–00:31 | 7s | The backyard — SMP | Ink background opens into a block landscape; the SMP label locks into place; grid parallax adds depth. |
| 00:31–00:38 | 7s | Shared moments | Three activity cards enter with slight stagger and rotation; cards settle into a clean row. |
| 00:38–00:45 | 7s | Closing lockup | The cottage symbol redraws; wordmark and closing line appear; hold on the final brand card. |

## Deliverable targets

- Main film: 1920×1080, 16:9, 45 seconds, native 120 FPS target (5,400 frames).
- Vertical adaptation: 1080×1920, 9:16, 45 seconds, redesigned framing rather than a simple crop.
- Editable source project with scene components, animation timing, and render instructions.
- Individual scene previews and original visual/audio assets.
- Original or properly licensed music and sound design.
