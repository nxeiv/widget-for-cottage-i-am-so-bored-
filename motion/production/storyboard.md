## Motion language

- Soft 0.3-second scene crossfades; no black-frame gaps.
- Small title rise and opacity reveal, restrained enough to keep the photographs primary.
- Gentle 2.5% camera push and low-amplitude photo drift.
- Slightly imperfect paper-card rotation, warm framing, and restrained tape accents.
- Fine animated monochrome grain with unique filter IDs per scene.
- Keep the dark scenes as breathing points rather than turning the film into a server advertisement.

## Sound direction (not yet produced)

The sound bed should be original and understated: a warm, simple instrumental texture with light room ambience. Small paper/tape handling sounds can accent a few cuts, while the backyard scene can have a very subtle environmental layer. Avoid loud trailer impacts, generic corporate swells, or copyrighted songs.
